THE RULES OF THE GAME

HOW TO PLAY OH MY GRID!

  1. Your goal is to complete a 3×3 Magic Square.
  2. You will be given numbers from 1 to 9.
  3. Place each number into the empty boxes of the grid.
  4. You can only use each number once.
  5. Every row, column, and diagonal must total exactly 15.
  6. Watch the sum counters to check your progress.
  7. Complete all eight lines to win!
